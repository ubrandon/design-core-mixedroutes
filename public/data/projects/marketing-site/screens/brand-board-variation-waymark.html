Color and line rules

Blaze marks the way. The route line, the primary button, and the going tag. If it points forward, it's blaze.
Everything is inked. 2px spruce borders on every card and button, with a hard offset shadow. No soft shadows, ever.
The route line is sacred. Always dashed, always one start dot and one end flag. It never decorates; it always connects two real things.
River means settled. Confirmed, joined, saved. Errors are blaze text with an underline, never a new red.

Wordmark & marks

MixedRoutes

The wordmark carries a small route line off its last letter: dot, dashes, flag. That mark alone works as the app icon, a sticker, and a favicon. Waypoint badges (circled numbers on marigold) count people, stops, and streaks.

Never: drone shots, empty landscapes without people, gear close-ups, or anything that reads as a hiking brand. The map language is about plans with friends, not the outdoors.

The signature: plan card

Inked border, hard offset shadow, caption printed below the photo, and the going tag tilted 6 degrees in the corner like a price sticker. The tilt plus the ink border is the tell for this identity.

Dark mode is the night trail

Night flips to spruce with kraft text, borders go kraft, and blaze stays blaze. The contour lines fade up slightly, like a map read by headlamp.
