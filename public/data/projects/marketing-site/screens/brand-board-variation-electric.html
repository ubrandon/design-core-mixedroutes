Color rules

Volt is a strobe, not a wash. It hits small: buttons, tags, underlines. Never a full volt background.
Ultra is the afterglow. Confirmed, joined, done. It never competes with volt in the same block.
Photos run hot. Saturation up, contrast up, flash-photo energy. No faded film looks.
Sharp corners everywhere. 4px max. This identity is cut, not rounded.

DISPLAY: SPACE GROTESK 700, ALL CAPS, TIGHT LEADING

Body: Space Grotesk regular at reading size. Labels, metadata, and timestamps switch to IBM Plex Mono, like a ticket stub or a setlist.

Wordmark & type rules

MixedRoutes

The wordmark sets in caps with Routes in volt. Headlines are all caps and break aggressively across lines. Mono is for facts only: dates, counts, places. Never set a headline in mono.

Imagery / flash and motion

Night, flash, mid-laugh
Small crews, big energy
Never: daylight stock scenes, pastel edits, soft-focus lifestyle shots, or corporate smiles. If a photo would work in a bank ad, it does not work here.

The signature: plan card

Same anatomy as the core card but cut sharp: 4px corners, a volt keyline, caps title, mono meta line, and a solid volt going tag. The keyline plus tag is the tell for this identity.

Light mode is the daylight exception

Chalk ground, same volt.

Daytime surfaces flip to chalk with blackout text. Volt and ultra stay exactly as they are, and photos keep the hot edit. It should still feel like night waiting to happen.

Test for any new surface: would it look at home on a gig poster, a wristband, or a story posted at 1am. If it feels calm, push it louder.
