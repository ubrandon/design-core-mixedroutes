Rules

The white edge is the brand. Any element that matters gets the 3px peel border and a hard drop. No edge, no importance.
Tilt with intent. Stickers rotate 2 to 7 degrees, one direction per cluster. Body text and inputs never tilt.
Cherry asks, taxi counts. Cherry is the invitation; taxi is the number: going counts, streaks, badges.
Flat color only. No gradients, no glass, no blur. Depth comes from the hard sticker shadow, nothing else.

Dark mode is midnight, same stickers

Midnight jacket.

Night swaps the ground to midnight and nothing else changes. Stickers keep their peel edges and full color; that is the point of stickers.

Test for any new surface: would a 24 year old actually put this sticker on their laptop. If the answer is no, redraw it until yes.
