Principles

The gradient is a verb.

Teal-to-blue appears only on things that move a plan forward: the CTA, the count pill, one accent stroke. Never as a background wash.

Warm ground, cool spark.

Swapping the cool gray page for warm paper changes everything. The gradient reads as energy against it instead of more screen.

Ember balances the blue.

The app's orange link color becomes the warm counterweight: labels, links, and small moments so the brand never feels cold.

Photography stays golden.

Photos are warm dusk and firelight, never blue-toned. The gradient and the imagery are opposites on purpose.
