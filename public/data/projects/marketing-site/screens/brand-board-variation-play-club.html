Rules of play

Color takes turns.One dominant, one supporting, one tiny surprise. Never make all five shout at once.
Round means social.Circles, pills, and soft corners cue people and conversation; outlines keep them crisp.
Copy talks back.Buttons say “I’m in,” empty states say “Who are we missing?” The product sounds like a friend.
Celebrate the yes.Small tilts, stickers, stamps, and color shifts reward momentum without confetti overload.
