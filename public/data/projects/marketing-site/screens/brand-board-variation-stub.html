Rules

Paper on dark. Tickets are always ticket paper on the dark ground. Never a dark ticket, never paper on paper.
Stamp is the ask. Buy-in moments only: join, invite, get the app. One stamp per view.
The perforation is real. Dashed line plus two punch notches, always. If a card has no stub, it is not a plan.
Valid means torn. Once you RSVP, the stub visually separates and the count ticks up. That animation is the brand.

Light mode is the matinee

Paper room, dark ink.

Daytime flips to ticket paper with house-lights ink, and tickets get a thin ink border to hold their shape. Stamp and valid stay exactly as they are.

Test for any new surface: does saying yes feel like getting a ticket, and does showing up feel like keeping the stub. If nothing tears, it is not this brand.
